Mamuthones prototype · flick test

Step, step, step, flick

Checks whether your phone can time a sharp wrist flick as well as a tap. Hold the phone firmly in both hands and play seated or standing.

  1. Beats 1–3: tap the left zone, then right, then left, on the click.
  2. Beat 4: flick both wrists sharply, like a Mamuthone snapping the bells on their back. Don't tap.
  3. The first bar is a count-in and isn't scored.
