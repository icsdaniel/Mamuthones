The Weight of Bells

A 45-second procession. Hold the phone in one hand: your thumb taps the steps, and shaking the phone up or down rings your bells. Headphones recommended.

  1. Calibrate: shake the phone 3 times up and 3 times down, so the game learns your shake.
  2. Play: hit each note as it reaches the line. Grey bars mean stand still: shaking there costs points.
