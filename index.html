The Weight of Bells

A 45-second procession. Hold the phone in both hands: your thumbs tap the three step buttons, and a sharp tilt up or down rings your bells. Headphones recommended.

  1. Calibrate: tilt the phone sharply 3 times up and 3 times down, so the game learns your move.
  2. Play: pick a song and hit each note as it reaches the line. Grey bars mean stand still: ringing there costs points.
