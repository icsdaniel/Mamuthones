# Sonazzos - Main Screen, Direction 2: "Controluce" (Against the Fire)

## Concept
One Mamuthone, cropped close and off-centre in three-quarter profile, back-lit by the Sant'Antonio bonfire of 16-17 January, the night the festa opens. A film still: the black visera reads by its silhouette, edges caught red and cream by the flames; the brown headscarf hoods it; a big sonazzu rises over the shoulder, two small ones hang on the fleece. Under the picture, the menu is a cream **bottom sheet**: a light, plain, modern mobile surface with hard-shadow keys.

## Why it differs
- **Night** shows the play street in perspective with a procession; **Su Manifestu** is a flat, frontal, framed poster with a centred mask and an arc of bells. Controluce is a single figure, **asymmetric and in profile**, cropped by the screen edges, lit from one side, with **no frame, no rope, no zig-zag border, no lanes, no ovals**.
- Both earlier screens are dark from top to bottom. Here the lower 42% is **cream**: picture on top, controls on paper below. The split is the whole layout.
- Buttons are rectangular **keys with a hard black shadow** that sink when pressed, not carved blocks or note ovals.

## Inks and light
- Five inks, 135x300 art px, 4x nearest. The glow is Bayer-dithered bands, no gradients.
- **Light comes from one side (the fire, right).** Shadow planes are black, surfaces turned to the fire are brown, ridges (brow, nose, lips, cheekbone) take warm grey, and the forward-most carving takes cream. Every edge facing the fire has a 1 px red rim. This lets a black mask read on a black night.
- On the sheet: red means "go" (Continue only); grey keys for secondary; black text and icons.

## Layout (art px)
- 0-173 hero, full bleed. Caption top-left "MAMOIADA / SU FOGU DE SANT'ANTONI" in 3x5.
- Logo "SONAZZOS": a heavy 13x17 face with 3 px strokes and chamfered corners, cream with a 2 px red extrusion and a black outline, sitting at the bottom of the picture like a film title.
- 174 sheet edge. Continue 119x47 (CONTINUE / STOP 4 at 2x / CARNIVAL SUNDAY, play arrow). Story and Songs 57x27 grey keys with icons. Small row: Learn, Ranks, and the Settings gear in the bottom-right corner. Every press target is in the lower half, under the thumb.

## Motion (120 BPM demo, 4 beats per 2 s loop)
On every beat (drop, hold 0.03 s, ease back over 0.15 s):
- the Mamuthone drops 2 px (the landing of the jump);
- the big back bell swings 8 degrees, the two small bells swing in opposite directions with their clappers lagging;
- the bonfire tongues flare 16% taller and the glow widens.
Embers drift and flames sway on periods dividing the 2 s loop, so it loops cleanly. The sheet never moves.

## States
Pressed: the key drops 3 px onto its shadow (the shadow disappears).
